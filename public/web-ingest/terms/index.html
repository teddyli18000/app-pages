web-ingest-drive

Terms of Use

Last updated: November 2026

web-ingest-drive is an independently maintained personal utility for transferring public Baidu Netdisk shares to a connected Google Drive account.

Use

Operate the utility only on shares you are allowed to access and copy, and follow applicable laws and platform rules. The operator controls the connected Google account and GitHub Actions workflow.

Availability

The tool is provided as-is without guarantees of uptime or successful transfers. It depends on external service availability.

Website

These pages are for information only. This site has no account registration, login, file-upload endpoint, payment form, or OAuth callback.

Privacy and contact

See the privacy policy. Contact the maintainer through GitHub Issues.
